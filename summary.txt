World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026:

Russia and Ukraine ended two days of US-brokered talks in Abu Dhabi, agreeing a large prisoner exchange but no deal on territory. US envoy Jared Kushner called it the biggest humanitarian release of prisoners, with numbers to be finalised the next day and the swap possibly within two to three weeks. (Reuters, AP)

Millions of workers joined Spain's first general strike in 14 years over housing costs. (AP)

At COP31 in Antalya, Turkey, UN chief Antonio Guterres warned leaders the slow climate response had put the world on a path to destruction, as 102 heads of state and government gathered. (UNFCCC, Just Security)

Iran's army said an oil tanker caught fire east of the Strait of Hormuz after hitting a naval mine in a prohibited zone. Britain's UKMTO confirmed a tanker incident but not the cause; Brent crude rose to about $93 a barrel. (Reuters)

Israeli strikes killed three Palestinians in Gaza City, including two brothers in a tent in the Sheikh Radwan neighbourhood, medics said. Israel's military said it struck militants. (Reuters)

Congo government data put confirmed Ebola cases at 11,042, with 5,387 deaths, making it the second-largest Bundibugyo-strain outbreak on record; the strain has no approved vaccine. (Reuters, AP)

US stocks closed higher after Tuesday's inflation-driven fall, the S&P 500 up 1.5%, led by CoreWeave, up about 26% after revenue rose 168% to $3.66 billion. Anthropic set a $70 to $78 range for 825 million shares in its IPO. (CNBC)

Israel's new parliament was sworn in on Tuesday without a new government, after an election stripped caretaker Prime Minister Benjamin Netanyahu of his majority. Former army chief Gadi Eisenkot, tasked with forming one, has until 6 December. (Reuters, AP)

Delhi's air stayed poor after Diwali, the average index at 299 on Wednesday morning, with the youngest pupils kept on hybrid classes. (Central Pollution Control Board)
